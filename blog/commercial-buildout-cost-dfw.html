Commercial Buildout Cost Guide for DFW

By Dillon Hubbard, OnPoint Pros · April 7, 2026

Commercial buildouts in DFW are priced very differently from residential remodels. Lease commencement deadlines, MEP coordination, and city permit timing all matter more than the finish package. Here's what actually drives the number.

Square Footage Is the Wrong Anchor

Most commercial tenants try to anchor on a $/sf number. That's a mistake. A 2,500 sf office buildout in a shell space with no existing MEP is a different project than a 2,500 sf restaurant buildout with a kitchen and grease trap, even though the square footage matches.

What you should anchor on instead: scope of MEP work, finish tier, kitchen/restroom complexity, and how much existing build-out you're reusing.

The Five Real Cost Drivers

1. MEP rough-in. Mechanical (HVAC), electrical, and plumbing are usually the largest single line item in any commercial buildout.

2. Finish tier. Polished concrete vs. carpet vs. hardwood. Painted drywall vs. wood feature walls. Each tier is a step up in real cost.

3. Kitchen or specialty rooms. Restaurant kitchens, dental sterile zones, salon wash stations, and medical exam rooms all carry specialty plumbing and electrical.

4. ADA upgrades. Older DFW buildings often need restroom retrofits, ramps, door widening, and signage to bring them up to current code.

5. Permitting timeline. City of Dallas, McKinney, Frisco, and Plano all have different turnaround times. The wait itself doesn't cost money — but missing your lease commencement date does.

After-Hours Premiums

If you're remodeling an occupied retail or restaurant space, work has to happen after hours so you don't lose revenue. Plan on a labor premium for night and weekend phases. It's worth it — every day you stay open during construction more than offsets the upcharge.
