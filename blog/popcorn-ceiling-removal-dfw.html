Popcorn Ceiling Removal in DFW: Cost, Process & What to Expect

By Dillon Hubbard, OnPoint Pros · July 1, 2026 · Updated October 25, 2026

A ceiling update involves more than removing texture. Start with the material condition, access, repairs and the finish you want, so the quote covers the complete job.

Popcorn ceiling removal is quoted for the actual ceiling, preparation and finish. Room size, height, existing coatings, repairs and access affect the work. OnPoint Pros drywall projects have a $1,000 minimum; confirm the full scope and schedule after assessment.

Before Any Ceiling Texture Is Disturbed

Some textured coatings can contain asbestos. Appearance or the home's age alone cannot confirm whether a material is safe to disturb. If the material is suspect, leave it undisturbed and arrange assessment and any sampling through a trained, accredited asbestos professional before scraping, sanding or drilling. EPA's homeowner guidance explains when inspection and professional handling are appropriate.

What to Include in the Work Plan

  • Assessment and scope: document the ceiling condition, coatings, repairs and any material-assessment requirements before choosing the removal or refinishing approach.
  • Access and protection: agree on furniture, floors, walls, fixtures, room isolation, equipment and cleanup responsibilities.
  • Surface preparation: identify how exposed joints, patches or damaged drywall will be assessed and repaired. Additional work needs an agreed scope and price.
  • Finish and paint: choose the texture or smooth finish, preparation, primer and paint. Discuss drying times and room access when setting the schedule.

What Changes the Quote

Measure the ceiling area and estimate its height. Share wide photos, close-ups of damage and whether there are existing painted coatings. Tall spaces, difficult access, surface repairs and the chosen finish can change preparation and equipment needs.

Ask the written quote to identify protection, preparation, repairs, texture or skim-coating work, primer, paint, fixture handling and cleanup. Confirm exclusions and how newly uncovered damage will be priced. A generic per-square-foot figure does not establish the cost of your complete project.

Choose the Finish and Plan Room Access

Bring photos of the adjoining ceilings and describe whether you prefer texture or a smoother appearance. Discuss the surface preparation and lighting, and ask about a sample where useful. Texture matching and paint appearance depend on the existing surfaces and agreed finish.

Confirm when the room can be used again and how the work fits around furniture, pets and other rooms. Preparation, repairs and drying or curing between stages affect the schedule. If new paint or flooring is also planned, discuss the sequence as one room scope.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does popcorn ceiling removal cost in DFW?

The quote depends on ceiling area and height, existing coatings, repairs, access, protection and the finish you choose. OnPoint Pros drywall projects have a $1,000 minimum. Send room photos and approximate dimensions so the complete scope can be reviewed.

Do I need to move out during popcorn removal?

Agree on room access, work-area isolation, ventilation and cleanup before scheduling. The arrangements depend on the work and any material-assessment findings. Do not assume an occupied room can remain in use during removal or refinishing.

Can the new finish match my other ceilings?

Share photos of the adjacent ceilings and discuss the texture and paint finish you want. Ask whether a sample is appropriate. Existing texture, repairs, lighting and paint can affect how closely the finished surfaces match.

Can I choose a smooth ceiling instead of texture?

Yes, discuss the desired finish before the quote. Smooth and textured finishes require different preparation, and lighting can make surface variation more noticeable. The written scope should identify the preparation, finish, primer and paint included.

Does a tall foyer ceiling change the quote?

Height and access can change the equipment, protection and work sequence. Send a wide room photo and the approximate ceiling height so those needs can be assessed with the surface area and repairs.

What if there is damage under the texture?

The surface may need joint repairs, patching or other preparation before finishing. Ask how concealed damage will be assessed, documented and priced before additional work proceeds. Resolve the cause of recurring moisture or damage before covering it.

Should ceiling work happen before new floors?

Discuss the sequence for the whole room. Completing ceiling preparation before new flooring may reduce protection and cleanup needs, but access, materials and drying times affect the plan. Keep the order of work in the agreed scope.
