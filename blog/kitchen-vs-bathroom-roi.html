Kitchen vs. Bathroom Remodel: Which Has Better ROI in DFW?

By Dillon Hubbard, OnPoint Pros · April 7, 2026 · Updated October 11, 2026

If you can only afford one big remodel, which should you do, kitchen or bathroom? It's the question every DFW homeowner asks. Honest answer: it depends on whether you're optimizing for resale or for daily life.

The Resale Answer

Across DFW, a well-executed kitchen remodel typically returns more on resale than a comparable bathroom remodel. Why: buyers tour the kitchen first, judge the home by the kitchen, and remember the kitchen.

But there's a catch, the kitchen ROI assumes the remodel matches the rest of the house. A luxury kitchen in a builder-grade home doesn't return well. A modest, well-done kitchen in a similarly modest home returns very well.

The Daily-Life Answer

If you cook every night, the kitchen wins. If you're the kind of person who escapes to a bath at the end of every day, the bathroom wins. ROI on lifestyle isn't measurable in dollars but it matters more than people admit.

Do Both, Strategically

If you can swing it, do them as one project rather than two. You'll save on contractor mobilization, permits get pulled together, and one walkthrough closes both. Two separate projects in DFW always cost more than one combined project of the same scope.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which one finishes faster?

Bathrooms, typically 2 to 4 weeks vs. 4 to 8 weeks for a full kitchen.

Do they require the same trades?

Mostly yes: both need plumbing, electrical, drywall, tile, paint. The kitchen adds cabinetry-as-a-major-line.

Is it cheaper to combine them?

Shared work may affect a quote, but there is no automatic combined-project discount. This article compares project types; OnPoint Pros currently focuses on full tile bathroom remodeling. Confirm any other scope before planning it.
