Room Refresh Before & After: North DFW Project Photos

By Dillon Hubbard · OnPoint Pros · Published August 9, 2026 · Updated October 25, 2026

A room refresh can combine wall repairs, painting, flooring and trim. Start by deciding which surfaces need work and which you want to keep, then compare quotes for that complete scope.

These are separate comparisons from our regional project portfolio, not stages of one renovation. They show room refinishing, garage drywall replacement and flooring updates. Use them to discuss your goals; your materials, preparation and schedule belong in your own written quote.

For work across several surfaces or rooms, see our home refresh and interior remodeling services, or send room photos and your project details.

Room Refinishing With the Existing Floor Kept

This comparison shows stone features and built-ins removed, followed by refinished walls and ceiling in a lighter palette. The original tile floor remains. It is an example of changing the room's surfaces without replacing every finish.

For your room, list the features to remove, surfaces to repair and walls, ceiling or trim to paint. Include occupied-room access and furniture protection when discussing the scope. See more painting project photos and request a painting quote.

Garage Drywall: Demolition to Finished Surfaces

The garage comparison starts after demolition, with exposed framing, and ends with finished walls and ceiling. The finished view comes from our project video. The original concrete floor remains; the comparison shows the walls and ceiling.

Send wide photos of the walls and ceiling, close-ups of damage, and approximate dimensions. Mention ceiling height, access and any known leak or moisture issue so the preparation can be assessed. Our drywall repair and installation service has a $1,000 project minimum. Send drywall project photos for the next step.

Bedroom and Stair Flooring Comparisons

The bedroom shows carpet replaced with wood-look plank flooring. The stair comparison shows hard-surface treads with white risers. These views may come from the same home. Material choices, preparation and installation timing are confirmed for your project.

Choose a product and installation method suitable for the room and subfloor. Include removal, preparation, transitions, stairs and trim when comparing the total scope. Review our LVP and engineered hardwood comparison, see more flooring projects, or request a flooring quote.

What to Include in a Room-Refresh Request

  • List the rooms, approximate dimensions and which walls, ceilings, floors and trim you want included.
  • Add a wide photo of each room plus close-ups of damaged or uneven areas. Include stairs, doorways and transitions for flooring work.
  • Describe what you want removed, kept or replaced, and any materials or colors you have already selected.
  • Tell us whether the home will be occupied, what furniture needs moving and your preferred timing.

Ask for preparation, removal, finish materials, protection, cleanup and work sequence in the written scope. The schedule and cost depend on the measured work and site conditions; these project photos are examples, not a fixed package.
