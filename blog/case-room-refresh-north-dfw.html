From a Patched Wall to a Full Room Refresh: Real North DFW Transformations

By Dillon Hubbard · OnPoint Pros · August 9, 2026

Almost every big transformation we finish in North DFW started as a small phone call. A doorknob through the drywall. A water stain spreading across a ceiling. One wall that needed paint. These are the photos from those calls — and what the rooms looked like when we were done.

It Starts With a Wall

This wall took water damage below the window — bad enough that the drywall had to come out down to the studs and insulation. We rebuilt it, matched the texture, reset the baseboard, and repainted. This is our bread and butter: when it's done, you can't find where the damage was.

But here's what happens next in most homes: the repaired section looks better than the rest of the room. Fresh texture and paint next to fifteen-year-old walls has a way of making homeowners look around and ask, "while you're here…"

Then the Paint

Paint is the fastest, cheapest transformation in the book. This living room had a dark red accent wall that shrank the space. Two coats of a bright neutral and clean lines later, the room reads twice as big — no walls moved, no plumbing touched.

Then the Floors

Floors are where a refresh becomes a transformation. Carpet out, waterproof luxury vinyl plank in — this room took one day. LVP handles kids, pets, spills, and DFW slab movement better than almost anything else, and it ties repaired walls and fresh paint into a room that reads brand-new.

Stairs count too — carpeted stairs to LVP treads is one of the most dramatic swaps we do:

Ceilings Change Everything

Nothing dates a North DFW home like a popcorn ceiling and a wallpaper border. Scraped, retextured, refinished — this is usually a one-to-two-day job per room, and it modernizes the space more than any single piece of furniture ever will.

And Sometimes It Becomes a Remodel

The refresh ladder has one more rung. Homeowners who've watched us transform a living room often bring us back for the bathroom — walk-in showers, tub-to-shower conversions, full renovations. Same crew, same standard:

Why One Crew Matters

Every photo above is our own crew's work — the same people who patch your drywall lay your floors and cut your paint lines. That's the difference between a refresh that takes a week and a relay race between three subcontractors that takes a month. One schedule, one project lead, one walkthrough.

A full room refresh — walls repaired, repainted, new floors, trim squared up — typically runs $5,000–$20,000 in North DFW depending on the room and finishes. Most start exactly like this article did: with one small repair.
