Drywall Repair Cost Guide for DFW Homeowners

By Dillon Hubbard, OnPoint Pros · July 1, 2026 · Updated October 25, 2026

OnPoint Pros has a $1,000 minimum for drywall projects. Include several repair areas in one request so the quote covers the whole job. The price depends on the area, access, board work, texture and painting you need.

What belongs in a drywall quote?

Repair area and new board. List damaged walls and ceilings, opened sections and any larger replacement work. Include wide photos as well as close-ups; a small opening may not show the full scope.

Height and access. A stairwell or tall ceiling needs a different work setup from an accessible wall. Send the approximate height and explain any access limits.

Seams and texture. Confirm whether the work includes hanging board, taping, finishing and matching the surrounding texture. The existing surface and lighting affect the finished appearance.

Primer and paint. Ask whether the quote covers only the repair, the whole wall or ceiling, or a complete room repaint. Older paint and different lighting can make a small touch-up noticeable.

Plan larger work as one scope

Garage drywall replacement, several damaged walls, ceiling repairs and room refinishing can be discussed together. Include every area you want assessed, even when the work is spread across more than one room.

If damage followed a leak or recurring movement, tell us what happened and whether the cause has been resolved. Photos help explain the visible work; access or hidden conditions may require more information before a price is confirmed.

Compare written quotes, not just patch prices

Check which areas, removal, new board, texture, priming, painting, protection and cleanup are included. Combining repair areas does not change the $1,000 minimum or guarantee a discount.

What to send for your drywall quote

Send your project ZIP code, wide photos of each room, close-ups of damaged areas and approximate measurements. Include all walls and ceilings you want repaired, their approximate heights and whether you want priming or painting in the same scope. Drywall projects have a $1,000 minimum.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to fix a hole in the wall in DFW?

OnPoint Pros has a $1,000 minimum for drywall projects. You can include several repair areas in one request; the total depends on the agreed scope, access, texture and painting. Send photos and rough measurements for a scope review and written quote.

Is drywall repair cheaper than replacing the whole wall?

That depends on the extent and condition of the damaged area. A localized repair and a full wall replacement involve different scopes; we assess the board, surrounding finish and access before recommending the work.

Can I request garage drywall and painting together?

Yes. Include the garage walls and ceiling, any demolition or new board needed, and the finish you want. The written quote should identify drywall, texture, primer and painting scope separately so you know what is included.
