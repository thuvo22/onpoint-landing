Drywall Repair Cost Guide for DFW Homeowners

By Dillon Hubbard, OnPoint Pros · July 1, 2026 · Updated July 9, 2026

“How much to fix a hole in my wall?” is the question we hear most. The honest answer is that drywall pricing is simple once you know the four things that move it. Here's how to read a drywall quote in DFW.

The Four Things That Drive Drywall Cost

1. Size of the damage. A doorknob hole and a 4-foot water-damaged section are different jobs. Small patches are the cheapest and often finish in one visit; bigger cutouts need backing, tape, and multiple mud coats.

2. Wall vs. ceiling. Ceiling work is slower and harder — everything is overhead, and gravity fights every coat. Expect ceiling repairs to run higher than the same-size wall patch.

3. Texture matching. Blending knockdown or orange-peel texture so the patch disappears is skilled work. A patch left flat is cheap and obvious; a properly matched patch takes more time and is what you're actually paying for.

4. Paint. Priming and painting the repair — or repainting the whole wall corner-to-corner for a perfect color match — adds to the job but is usually worth it.

Typical DFW Drywall Jobs

Small patches (holes, cracks, anchor scars) are the most affordable and common. Ceiling repairs and water-damage cutouts are quoted per area because they involve removal, drying, and refinishing. Popcorn ceiling removal is priced per room. Whole-room retexture or new sheetrock install for an addition is a larger, per-project number.

Rather than guess, we ask for a photo. A picture of the damage plus a rough measurement tells us almost everything we need to give a firm price.

How to Avoid Overpaying

Get an upfront, written price — not a vague “we'll see when we get there.” Ask whether texture matching and paint are included. And bundle small jobs: if you have three holes in different rooms, one visit is far cheaper than three.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to fix a hole in the wall in DFW?

Small holes and patches start at a low flat rate and often finish in a single visit. Text us a photo and we'll give you an exact price before we start.

Is drywall repair cheaper than replacing the whole wall?

Almost always. Cutting out and patching the damaged section, then texture-matching, is far cheaper than tearing out and rehanging a full wall — and just as invisible when done right.

Does insurance cover drywall repair?

For sudden water or storm damage it often does. We can document the damage and work with your adjuster's scope on covered repairs.
