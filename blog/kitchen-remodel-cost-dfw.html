Kitchen Remodel Cost Guide for DFW Homeowners

By Dillon Hubbard, OnPoint Pros · April 7, 2026

Every DFW homeowner asks the same question first: what's a kitchen remodel actually going to cost? Honest answer — it depends on six things, and most contractors won't tell you which one is driving your number up. Here's a breakdown.

The Six Things That Drive a DFW Kitchen Remodel Price

Talk to ten DFW kitchen remodelers and you'll get ten different quotes for the same project. The reason isn't shady pricing — it's that contractors include and exclude wildly different things. Before you can compare quotes, you have to understand what's actually moving the number.

Here are the six cost drivers that matter, in order of impact:

1. Scope. A cosmetic refresh (cabinet paint, new countertops, new backsplash, same layout) is a fundamentally different project from a gut remodel that moves walls and plumbing. Don't compare apples and oranges.

2. Cabinetry tier. Stock cabinets, semi-custom, and fully custom can vary by 3x for the same kitchen footprint. This is usually the single biggest line item.

3. Countertops. Quartz, granite, marble, and butcher block all live in different price brackets. Marble is also priced by slab — an oversized island in a single slab costs more than two smaller pieces.

4. Layout changes. Moving the sink, range, or fridge means moving plumbing, gas, and electrical. Removing a wall means engineering for a load-bearing beam. Both add real cost.

5. Appliances. Built-in refrigerators, panel-ready dishwashers, and pro-grade ranges can easily double an appliance package over standard versions.

6. Lighting and electrical. Recessed cans, pendants, under-cabinet LED, and added circuits all have a real labor cost.

How to Read a DFW Kitchen Remodel Quote

Most quotes you'll get from DFW remodelers fall into one of two formats: a single bottom-line number with vague "allowances," or a detailed line-item breakdown. Always ask for the second.

A real quote should show: demo, framing, plumbing rough-in, electrical, drywall and texture, cabinet labor, countertop install, tile, fixtures, appliances install, paint, finish carpentry, cleanup, and permits. Each as its own line.

If a contractor refuses to break it down, that's a yellow flag. They're either hiding margin or they don't actually know their costs.

Where DFW Homeowners Overspend

We see the same overspends across DFW kitchens almost every week:

Custom cabinets when semi-custom would do. Unless you have a truly weird footprint, semi-custom cabinets in a quality plywood-box build will give you 90% of the look at 60% of the cost.

Marble countertops in homes with kids. Marble stains. Always. Quartz looks 95% as good and never needs sealing.

Pro-grade appliances they'll never use the features of. A 48-inch dual-fuel range with six burners and a griddle is gorgeous, but if you cook two nights a week, it's overkill.

Moving plumbing for design instead of function. Sink relocation is one of the priciest line items. Sometimes the original location is fine and you're paying for a design crush, not a functional improvement.

Where DFW Homeowners Underspend (and Regret It)

On the other side, here's where we see homeowners try to save money and regret it within a year:

Cheap cabinet boxes. Particle-board boxes will fail at the hinges within 5-7 years. Always insist on plywood-box construction.

Skipping under-cabinet lighting. Adding it during the rough-in is cheap. Adding it after the tile backsplash is in is expensive.

Cheap faucets. The faucet is the part of your kitchen you touch the most. A no-name faucet will leak or corrode within two years.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why won't you give me a price range over the phone?

Because phone ballparks for kitchens are almost always wrong. A 12x14 kitchen with stock cabinets and same-footprint plumbing is a completely different project from a 12x14 kitchen with custom cabinets, a moved sink, and a removed wall. We come measure for free.

Should I buy my own appliances and have you install?

Yes, this is fine and we do it often. We'll handle the install as part of the project. Just buy them before demo so we know exact dimensions for cabinet planning.

Do I need to move out during a kitchen remodel?

No, but the kitchen will be offline for weeks. Most clients set up a temporary kitchenette with a microwave and mini-fridge in the dining or laundry room.

How long does a DFW kitchen remodel take?

Most full kitchen remodels run 4 to 8 weeks depending on cabinet lead times and scope. Cosmetic refreshes can be 2 to 3 weeks.
