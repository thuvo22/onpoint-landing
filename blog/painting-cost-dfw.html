Whole-Home Painting Cost Guide for DFW

By Dillon Hubbard, OnPoint Pros · April 7, 2026 · Updated October 25, 2026

A painting quote should explain which surfaces are included and how they will be prepared. For a multi-room repaint, compare the full scope: walls, ceilings, trim, doors, repairs, protection and cleanup.

What changes the price of a repaint?

Rooms and surfaces. Floor area alone does not describe the work. List each room and whether the quote includes ceilings, closets, trim and doors.

Wall condition. Cracks, holes, rough texture and peeling finishes can require different preparation. Include repairs in the scope before comparing paint prices.

Access and occupancy. Tall walls, stairwells, furniture and occupied rooms affect how the work is planned. Ask which moving and protection tasks are included.

Colors and products. Existing colors, the selected coating and the condition of the surface affect preparation and application. Confirm the product, finish and application scope in writing.

Compare the same work in every quote

Ask for the surfaces, repairs, preparation, priming, paint products and cleanup to be listed. Clarify whether furniture handling, trim repairs and disposal are included or separate. Cabinet refinishing and exterior work should have their own agreed scope rather than being assumed part of an interior repaint.

For walls that need both repair and paint, discuss the full area together. A small touch-up may remain visible against older paint; ask whether repainting the full wall or room is appropriate.

What to send for your painting quote

Send your project ZIP code, wide photos of each room, close-ups of damaged areas and approximate measurements. List walls, ceilings, trim and doors you want painted, plus any drywall work and whether the home will be occupied.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can drywall repairs be included in the painting project?

Yes. Include the cracks, holes or texture repairs in your request so we can assess the repair and painting scope together. Drywall projects have a $1,000 minimum; the combined work receives its own written quote.

Do I need to move out for an interior repaint?

Tell us whether the home will be occupied and which rooms must stay usable. Access, protection, ventilation and the work sequence should be agreed before work begins; the arrangement depends on the project.

How long does a whole-home interior repaint take?

The schedule depends on the rooms, surface repairs, preparation, selected products and access. Ask for the planned sequence and drying time for your specific project rather than relying on a single whole-home estimate.
