Tub-to-Shower Conversion Cost & Guide for DFW Homeowners

By Dillon Hubbard, OnPoint Pros · July 1, 2026 · Updated July 9, 2026

Swapping a rarely-used garden tub for a walk-in shower is our single most-requested bathroom project in DFW — and for good reason. Here's what it costs, how it works, and when it's worth it.

Why DFW Homeowners Convert

Most DFW homes built from the '90s through the 2000s came with a big garden tub in the master bath that almost nobody uses. It eats floor space, it's awkward to climb into, and it collects dust. Converting it to a walk-in shower reclaims that space and modernizes the whole room.

It's especially popular with empty-nesters and anyone planning to age in place — a curbless walk-in shower with a bench and grab bars is safer and far more usable than stepping over a tub wall.

What Drives the Cost

Shower size and tile. A basic alcove conversion is the most affordable; expanding the footprint or using floor-to-ceiling large-format tile costs more.

Glass. A frameless glass enclosure is beautiful but pricier than semi-frameless or a simple glass panel.

Plumbing. Reusing the existing drain and valve location keeps costs down; relocating them adds labor.

Extras. Benches, niches, linear drains, body sprays, and curbless entries each add to the number — but are where the spa feel comes from.

The Process, Step by Step

We remove the old tub, inspect and adjust the plumbing, frame and waterproof the new shower with a Schluter system, tile, set the glass, and finish. Most conversions take about one to two weeks depending on tile and glass lead times.

The waterproofing step is the one you can't see and can't skip — it's the difference between a shower that lasts decades and one that leaks into the subfloor in a few years.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a tub-to-shower conversion cost in DFW?

It depends on shower size, tile, and glass, but it's one of the more budget-friendly bathroom upgrades because it reuses the existing space and often the existing plumbing. We give a written line-item quote after a free on-site visit.

How long does a tub-to-shower conversion take?

Most conversions take one to two weeks, depending on tile selection and glass lead time. We give you a schedule up front.

Can you add grab bars and a bench for aging in place?

Yes. Curbless entries, built-in benches, comfort-height fixtures, and properly-blocked grab bars are some of our most common conversion requests.
