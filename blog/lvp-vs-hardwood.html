LVP vs. Engineered Hardwood: Which Holds Up in DFW?

By Dillon Hubbard, OnPoint Pros — Licensed GC · April 7, 2026

Luxury vinyl plank (LVP) and engineered hardwood are the two most popular flooring options in DFW remodels. Both are great. Here's how to pick.

LVP Wins for Family Life

LVP is waterproof, scratch-resistant, doesn't dent from dropped objects, and tolerates pet accidents without staining. For DFW families with kids and dogs, it's almost always the right answer.

Modern LVP also looks remarkably close to real wood — close enough that most guests can't tell.

Engineered Hardwood Wins for Look and Resale

Engineered hardwood has a real wood veneer over a stable plywood core. It feels like wood underfoot, looks like wood up close, and is recognized as a premium material on a resale listing.

It's not waterproof — spills should be wiped quickly, and it's not ideal for bathrooms or laundry rooms. It scratches more easily than LVP. But the look is hard to beat.

Don't Even Consider Solid Hardwood in DFW

Solid 3/4" hardwood expands and contracts with humidity. DFW summers and winters are brutal on it. Engineered hardwood is the modern standard.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is cheaper?

LVP is typically lower per square foot in both material and labor.

Can either go in a bathroom?

LVP yes, engineered hardwood not recommended.

How long do they last?

Quality LVP lasts 15–25 years. Engineered hardwood can last 30+ years and may be refinished once or twice.
