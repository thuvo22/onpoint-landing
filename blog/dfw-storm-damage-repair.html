DFW Storm Damage: When to Call a GC vs. Insurance Restoration

By Dillon Hubbard, OnPoint Pros · April 7, 2026

Storm season in DFW guarantees one thing: a lot of homeowners filing claims. Here's how to think about who actually does the repair work.

Insurance Restoration Companies

Insurance restoration companies are designed to work directly with insurance carriers. They handle the paperwork, deal with adjusters, and get paid through the claim. The upside: less paperwork for you. The downside: they're often national chains with rotating crews and a focus on volume over craftsmanship.

Remodeling Companys

A local GC can do the same work, often with more attention to detail, but you'll do more of the insurance paperwork yourself. The upside: better workmanship, in-house crew, and a real warranty. The downside: more coordination effort.

The Right Move for Most DFW Homeowners

For minor storm damage — drywall patches, small ceiling repairs, a single room — call a GC. The work is fast and the quality is better.

For major damage — multiple rooms, structural issues, full-room rebuild — the calculation is closer. Sometimes restoration companies make sense; sometimes a GC is still the better call.
