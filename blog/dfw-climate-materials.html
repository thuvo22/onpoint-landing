Building Materials That Actually Survive a DFW Climate

By Dillon Hubbard, OnPoint Pros — Licensed GC · April 7, 2026

DFW is hard on building materials. 105°F summers, 30°F winters, hail, expansive clay soil, and constant humidity swings will eat anything cheap. Here's what survives.

Exterior Paint

Cheap exterior paint fades in 18 months under DFW UV. Spec premium acrylic from Sherwin-Williams or Benjamin Moore in their exterior lines. The product cost difference is small. The lifespan difference is massive.

Caulk

Painter's caulk on exterior trim fails in DFW heat cycles. Spec a polyurethane or silicone-blend caulk for any exterior application.

Tile Underlayment

DFW slab homes move with the clay soil underneath. Tile installed without a decoupling membrane will telegraph cracks within a year. Always spec Schluter Ditra or equivalent under tile floors.

Cabinet Construction

Particle-board cabinets in a DFW kitchen will swell at the toe-kick within 5 years from humidity. Plywood-box construction is the standard.

Roofing Materials

Class 4 impact-resistant shingles cost more upfront but survive DFW hail seasons. Most insurance carriers also offer a discount for them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does DFW soil matter for my flooring?

Expansive clay moves with moisture. Without proper underlayment, that movement transfers up through tile and grout, causing cracks.

Do I need impact glass for windows?

Not legally, but it helps in hail-prone areas of DFW.

What's the biggest material upgrade for resale value?

Exterior paint and roof — the things buyers see first.
