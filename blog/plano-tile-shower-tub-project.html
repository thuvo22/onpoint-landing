Plano tile shower and tub remodel

A tiled walk-in shower, frameless glass, a built-in tub surround and new bathroom flooring come together in this OnPoint Pros project. Compare the demolition-stage room with the completed remodel below.

An in-progress view of the Plano bathroom

What changed in this room

The demolition photo shows the tub removed, open tub framing and stripped walls beside it. The completed Plano bathroom combines a separate built-in tub and walk-in shower with a dark vanity and light marble-look floor tile.

Dark shower tile and clear glass

Dark tile covers the shower walls and floor. A recessed wall niche provides a place for shower products, and the frameless glass keeps the shower tile visible from the rest of the room.

A built-in tub beside the shower

The white tub sits within a dark deck and surround below the window. The finished room includes both bathing options, with the tub next to the shower enclosure.

Light flooring with dark finishes

The marble-look bathroom floor contrasts with the dark shower tile, tub surround and vanity. This photo shows the finished combination; it can help you compare light and dark surfaces when planning your own room.

Planning a similar tile shower and tub?

Do you want to keep a separate bathtub?

Decide how you want to use the room before choosing finishes. This Plano example includes a built-in tub and shower; our Allen project combines a freestanding tub and shower, while our McKinney project replaced a tub with a tile shower. Your room measurements and priorities guide the layout.

Which details should you decide together?

Compare the shower tile, bathroom floor, tub surround, vanity and glass as a complete room. Bring examples of finishes you like, photos of the current bathroom and any storage or access needs to the planning conversation.

What belongs in your project quote?

Agree on the layout, selected finishes and included work before comparing prices. Ask how any changes to the tub, shower, plumbing locations or room condition affect your scope. The Primary Bath Blueprint develops your room measurements, layout options, selections and detailed quote.

Project investment and your next step

Our published bathroom project gallery lists this Plano remodel at an approximate investment of $30,000–$40,000. It is an example of one room and scope, not a fixed price for another bathroom. Room size, layout changes and finish selections affect your quote.
