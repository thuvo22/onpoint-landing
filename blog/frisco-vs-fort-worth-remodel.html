Remodeling in Frisco vs. Fort Worth: What's Different

By Dillon Hubbard, OnPoint Pros · April 7, 2026

Frisco and Fort Worth are both technically DFW — but they're very different remodeling environments. Here's what changes between them.

Housing Stock

Frisco is overwhelmingly newer construction (2000s and later). Most projects are first-refresh remodels of homes that were built well but with builder-grade finishes.

Fort Worth has every era — from 1920s craftsman bungalows in Fairmount to brand-new construction north of Loop 820. Each era has its own remodeling quirks.

HOA Rules

Frisco neighborhoods are heavily HOA-managed. Most exterior changes require submittal and approval. Build that into your timeline.

Fort Worth has HOAs in newer developments but most older neighborhoods don't have them. More freedom, less paperwork.

Permit Timelines

Frisco generally has faster permit turnaround than Fort Worth. Plan for 1–2 weeks longer in Fort Worth on bigger projects.

Design Preferences

Frisco trends modern transitional — white shaker, quartz, brass or matte black fixtures, white oak floors.

Fort Worth has more variety — modern in newer neighborhoods, classic in historic districts, ranch-modern in mid-century pockets.
