Our Process

Plan Your Bathroom Remodel, Start to Finish

Full bathroom remodels start at $20,000. Begin with a planning call, then develop the scope through a paid Primary Bath Blueprint. Repair requests follow a separate quoting path.

01

Planning Call

Tell us your location, goals, approximate budget and timing. We will confirm whether your full bathroom project is a fit. Full bathroom remodels start at $20,000; most full-tile primary bathrooms are $25,000–$45,000 depending on scope and finishes.

02

Paid Primary Bath Blueprint

The in-home working session covers measurements, layout options, finish selections and a fixed-price quote for the proposed scope. The fee is usually $1,000, fully credited toward your remodel if you move forward. We confirm the fee and what is included before you book.

03

Written Scope & Preparation

Review the inclusions, exclusions, selections, payment terms and schedule before construction. Our team coordinates materials for the agreed scope before demolition. Permit requirements and trade responsibilities depend on the work and are confirmed during planning.

04

Build & Agreed Changes

Your project lead coordinates the approved work and communicates progress. If concealed damage or a requested change affects the plan, review the scope, price and schedule change in writing before that additional work proceeds.

05

Walkthrough & Handover

Walk through the completed scope with your project lead, note any remaining items and review care information. Refer to your project agreement for the schedule, warranty terms and any applicable completion guarantee.

Drywall, Painting & Interior Repairs

For repairs, send photos, rough measurements and the full list of areas needing attention. Drywall projects have a $1,000 minimum. Other repair pricing depends on the requested service; we confirm the scope and any visit requirements before booking.
