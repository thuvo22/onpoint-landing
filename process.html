Behind the tile

Shower waterproofing and a documented flood test

Our tile crews build a connected waterproofing system behind the shower tile and under the shower floor. We select the assembly for your shower and install it according to the system instructions and applicable local requirements.

  • Backer and walls: cement backer board or approved foam board in wet areas.
  • Waterproofing membrane: a continuous sheet or liquid-applied system on the shower walls, niche, bench and curb.
  • Shower pan: a properly sloped base with a waterproof liner or pan system connected to the drain and wall waterproofing.

24-hour flood test for new tiled showers

Before we install shower floor tile, we plug the drain, fill the pan with water and verify that the level does not drop during a 24-hour flood test. We photo-document the test and keep the photographs in your job file.

You are welcome to see the test in person. The waterproofing is installed by our tile crews.

Licensed trades, permits and inspections

OnPoint Pros works with licensed master plumbers and licensed electricians. During planning, we confirm the plumbing and electrical work included in your bathroom scope.

Who handles permits?

For projects requiring permits, OnPoint Pros checks your city's requirements, applies for permits under our registration, and coordinates and meets inspectors for the required inspections. Moving plumbing, electrical or structural walls can trigger permit requirements; we check the actual requirements for your project.

Are permit fees included?

Expected permit and inspection fees appear as a separate line item in your fixed-price quote and are included in the total before you sign. If the city changes its fees, we show you the actual amount and adjust only that line.

After your remodel

3-year written workmanship warranty

Every full-tile bathroom remodel we build comes with a 3-year written workmanship warranty. If something fails because of how we installed it during that period, we fix it at our cost.

What it covers

  • Tile and stone installation: floors, shower walls, niches and curbs.
  • Grout and setting materials related to our tile work.
  • Shower pan and waterproofing we installed.
  • Framing, backer board and blocking related to the shower.
  • Plumbing connections we disturbed inside the bathroom, including valves, drains and supply lines we touched.

What's not covered

  • Normal wear and tear, cosmetic hairline grout cracks, or caulk that needs periodic refresh.
  • Damage from neglect, abuse or improper cleaning products.
  • Movement or settling of the home's structure.
  • Leaks caused by plumbing or roofing outside the bathroom, or by work others did after us.
  • Changes, repairs or additions done by anyone other than OnPoint Pros.

If you see something you're unsure about, call or text (469) 617-5235 and we'll come take a look. If it's on us, we make it right.

Manufacturer warranties

Materials such as faucets, fixtures, tile, glass and fans carry their own manufacturer warranties. We register and pass those through to you and provide all documentation at project wrap-up.

If there's a product defect, we help you submit the claim and coordinate replacement during the first year. After that, the manufacturer continues coverage under its own terms.
