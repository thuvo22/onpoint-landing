Our Process

A Predictable DFW Remodel, Start to Finish

No vague promises. No disappearing crews. No surprise change-orders. Here's exactly what every OnPoint Pros project looks like.

01

Free On-Site Estimate

We come to your DFW home, measure, look at the space, and listen to what you actually want. No high-pressure sales call. Most visits take 45 minutes to an hour. We don't do phone "ballparks" because they're almost always wrong.

02

Written Line-Item Quote

A detailed scope, itemized materials, fixture list, and a milestone-based timeline — in writing — before we ask for a deposit. You see every line. No vague allowances. No "TBD" hiding a future surprise.

03

Material Selection & Permits

We help you pick cabinets, tile, fixtures, and finishes — at our preferred DFW suppliers or wherever you want. Then we pull every required city permit on your behalf. It's not your job to figure out city hall.

04

Build Phase

Floor protection from the front door to the work area. In-house crew on site daily. Daily progress photos texted directly to you. One project lead you can call or text anytime. We close walls only after rough-in inspections pass.

05

Walkthrough & Warranty

You walk the project with us, list anything that needs touch-up, and we fix it before the final invoice. Workmanship warranty in writing on every job. If something we installed fails later, we come back and fix it. That's the deal.

That's the whole process. No surprises.

It's why our clients refer their friends.
