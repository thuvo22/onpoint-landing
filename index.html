The old kitchen had honey oak cabinets from the 90s, white appliances past their prime, dated laminate counters, and a single overhead light that made the whole space feel dark and cramped. Cooking felt like a chore, and the layout wasted valuable counter space.

Now, natural light bounces throughout the room. The homeowners have better storage, more usable counter space, and a kitchen that actually makes them want to cook. Plus, modern kitchens like this consistently add real value when it's time to sell.

Kitchen Remodel FAQs

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

Most complete kitchen remodels take 4–8 weeks once materials arrive and work begins. Timelines depend on scope, material lead times, and whether we're moving walls or plumbing. We'll give you a realistic range during your consultation.

What does a kitchen remodel cost?

Kitchen remodels vary widely based on size, materials, and scope. Rather than quote a number that may not apply to your project, we prefer to see your space and understand your goals first. We'll provide a detailed written proposal with clear pricing before any work starts.

What's included in a full kitchen remodel?

Typically: demo, new cabinets, countertops, backsplash, flooring, lighting, plumbing fixtures, painting, and installation of your appliances. We detail everything in your proposal so you know exactly what's covered.

Do you help with design and material selection?

Yes. We provide design support and guide you through selecting cabinets, counters, tile, fixtures, and finishes. We'll recommend options that fit your style and budget without making the process overwhelming.

Do you handle permits?

When permits are required (e.g., for electrical or structural changes), we handle the permit process. We'll let you know during planning if your project needs one.

How do you handle dust and cleanup?

We protect your floors and adjacent areas, use dust barriers during demo, and clean up daily. At project completion, we do a thorough deep clean so you can start enjoying your new kitchen right away.

What warranty do you offer?

We provide a workmanship warranty covering our labor. Products like cabinets and countertops have their own manufacturer warranties, and we'll give you clear documentation on all of it.

How will I stay updated during my project?

You'll have a single point of contact and receive updates at agreed milestones—typically weekly. If questions come up, we respond quickly. No ghosting, no radio silence.
