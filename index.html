Questions Homeowners Ask

Frequently Asked Questions

Are you a fully insured remodeler in Texas?

Yes. OnPoint Pros is fully insured and bonded and carries general liability and workers' compensation insurance. We provide proof of both before any project starts and pull all required permits for your city.

How much does a kitchen or bathroom remodel cost in DFW?

Every project is different, but the biggest cost drivers are: scope (cosmetic refresh vs. layout change), cabinetry tier, countertop material, and whether plumbing or electrical needs to move. We give every homeowner a free, written estimate with line-item pricing — no guessing, no upsells. Read our cost guides in the blog for category-by-category breakdowns.

Do you handle small jobs or only full remodels?

Both. OnPoint Pros runs full kitchen, bathroom, and whole-home remodels — but we also send our handyman crew for the smaller stuff most contractors ignore: drywall patches, fixture swaps, mounting, punch lists, and seasonal repairs.

What DFW cities do you serve?

We work across the North Dallas metroplex including Frisco, Plano, McKinney, Allen, Prosper, The Colony, Little Elm, McKinney, Allen, Prosper, and Dallas. If you're in DFW, give us a call — we'll let you know on the spot whether we can route a crew to your address.

How long does a typical remodel take?

A bathroom refresh typically runs 2–3 weeks. A full kitchen remodel runs 4–8 weeks depending on cabinet lead times and scope. Whole-home renovations run longer. Every estimate we give includes a written, milestone-based timeline before you sign anything.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes — every estimate is free and on-site. We don't do "ballpark" quotes over the phone because they're almost always wrong. We come to your home, measure, talk through what you want, and follow up with a written line-item quote.
