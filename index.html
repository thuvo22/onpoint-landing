Before you get started

Can I still hire you for drywall or a small repair?

Yes. We continue to handle drywall repair, texture matching, painting and interior updates. Send a repair request with a description and optional photos. These jobs are quoted separately from full bathroom remodels.

What is the budget for a full bathroom remodel?

Most full-tile primary bathroom remodels are $25,000–$45,000, with a $20,000 minimum. Size, layout and finishes affect the final quote. That minimum applies to full bathroom remodels, not drywall or repair work.

How does bathroom planning work?

Start with a planning call. If the project is a fit, the next step is the paid Primary Bath Blueprint: an in-home design and layout session, usually $1,000 and fully credited toward your remodel if you move forward. We confirm the fee before booking.

Where do you work?

We are based in Allen. Our core bathroom area is Allen, Plano, Frisco and McKinney, with repairs and interior updates available in surrounding North Dallas communities. Share your ZIP code so we can confirm coverage and availability.
