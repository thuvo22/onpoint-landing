Frisco Flooring FAQ

How much does flooring installation cost in Frisco?

Flooring is priced per square foot and varies by material — laminate and carpet are most affordable, LVP is the popular mid-range, hardwood and tile are premium. We give a firm per-square-foot quote after measuring, including any subfloor prep.

Do you level the subfloor first?

Yes. Frisco slabs are rarely flat because of North Texas soil movement. We check flatness and self-level or fix squeaks before installing — the step most installers skip and the reason floors fail early.

What's the best flooring for Frisco homes?

For most homes with kids or pets, waterproof luxury vinyl plank (LVP) is the best value. Engineered hardwood handles our humidity better than solid wood, and porcelain tile is best for wet areas.

Can you move furniture and haul off the old floor?

Yes — we handle furniture moving, tear-out, and haul-away of the old flooring as part of the job so you're not left with the mess.
