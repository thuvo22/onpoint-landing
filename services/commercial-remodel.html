Commercial Remodeling FAQ

Do you work with our landlord and architect?

Yes. We coordinate directly with your landlord, property manager, architect, and MEP engineer. We've built out spaces in dozens of DFW commercial properties.

Can you work nights and weekends?

Yes. We routinely run after-hours phases for occupied retail and restaurants so the business stays open during construction.

Do you handle ADA compliance?

Yes — restroom retrofits, ramps, door widening, signage, and full ADA path-of-travel reviews are part of our commercial work.

How quickly can you start?

Lead time depends on permit turnaround in your city. Once permits are pulled, demo can typically start within a week.
