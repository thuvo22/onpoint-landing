Plan the Room as One Scope

Start with the full room, not just the most visible damaged spot. List the walls, ceilings, paint, flooring and trim you want included, and identify finishes or features you plan to keep. Several rooms can be reviewed together when they share preparation or finish needs.

Existing conditions shape the work. Show us cracks, old patches, stains and uneven floors, and tell us whether the cause of any leak or recurring damage has been resolved. Surface preparation, floor preparation and finish choices should be agreed before the work is priced.

Discuss the sequence before scheduling. Drying or curing time, material availability, furniture, access and occupied rooms all affect the plan. The written scope should identify the preparation, materials, work areas and cleanup included, along with any items left for a later phase.

What a Refresh Can Include

Scope it to one room or the whole house, you pick what's in and what waits.

Walls & Ceilings

Include damaged areas, old patches and texture changes in the room scope. Confirm the repair and preparation needed before priming or painting.

Interior Painting

Plan walls, ceilings, doors and trim together. Share the current and desired colors, surface condition and finish preferences.

Flooring

Compare flooring for your room and household needs. Existing floor removal, subfloor condition, moisture requirements and transitions belong in the estimate.

Trim & Room Finishes

Include baseboards, door casings and other agreed trim work with the paint and flooring plan. Specify what will stay, be refinished or be replaced.

Multi-Room Updates

List connected rooms together so preparation, materials and the order of work can be reviewed as one project. Identify any phases you want quoted separately.

Preparation & Access

Share ceiling heights, furniture and occupied-room needs. Agree on protection, access and cleanup before the schedule is set.

Home Refresh Questions DFW Homeowners Ask

What can a home refresh include?

A refresh can combine drywall repairs or retexturing, interior painting, flooring and trim in one room or several rooms. List the surfaces and finishes you want to change and what should stay. The written scope confirms what is included.

How much does a home refresh cost?

The quote depends on the rooms, repairs, preparation, selected finishes, floor condition and access. Drywall work has a $1,000 project minimum; a combined home refresh is priced for its complete agreed scope. Send photos and approximate dimensions for an initial review, with a site visit if needed before the quote is confirmed.

How long does a room refresh take?

Timing depends on repairs, surface and floor preparation, material availability, installation and drying or curing needs. Tell us which rooms are occupied and your preferred timing. Confirm the sequence and schedule once the scope and materials have been reviewed.

Can we plan the work in phases?

Tell us which rooms or finishes are priorities now and which may wait. We can discuss the order of work and identify phases in the written scope so you understand what each phase includes before deciding to proceed.

What should I send with my request?

Send wide photos of each room, close-ups of damage, approximate dimensions and a list of walls, ceilings, paint, flooring and trim to include. Tell us what you want to keep, whether the home is occupied and when you hope to begin. The request starts a scope discussion; it does not book or charge you for work.
