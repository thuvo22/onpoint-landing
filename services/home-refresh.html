Most Refreshes Start With One Small Repair

Here's how it usually goes: a homeowner calls us about a drywall patch, a ceiling stain, or one room that needs paint. While we're there, they point at the floors they've been living with since the house was built, the trim that never got finished, the accent wall that seemed like a good idea in 2012.

Fixing the wall and stopping there is fine — we do it every week. But patched drywall next to tired paint and dated floors only gets a room halfway back. A refresh finishes the thought: repair the walls, repaint properly, lay the new floor, square up the trim — in the right order, so nothing gets done twice.

Because one crew handles all of it, you skip the part where a painter blames the drywall guy and the flooring sub blames both. One project lead, one schedule, one walkthrough at the end.

Home Refresh Questions DFW Homeowners Ask

What is a home refresh and how is it different from a full remodel?

A refresh updates the surfaces and finishes — drywall, paint, flooring, trim, hardware, fixtures — without moving walls or plumbing. It captures most of a remodel's visual impact at a fraction of the cost and time. Most North DFW refreshes run $5,000–$20,000 depending on rooms and finishes.

How long does a room refresh take?

A single room — walls repaired, painted, new flooring, new trim — typically takes 3 to 7 working days. Multi-room or whole-home refreshes usually land between 2 and 4 weeks.

Can we start with one repair and grow from there?

That's how most of our projects actually start. We come out for a drywall patch or paint job, scope the bigger picture while we're there, and you get one written quote to decide what happens now versus later. No pressure — the repair alone is always fine.

Do you handle everything with one crew?

Yes — drywall, texture, paint, flooring, trim, and finish work are done by our own crew with one project lead. You never coordinate between separate contractors.
