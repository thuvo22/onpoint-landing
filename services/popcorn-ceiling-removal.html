How Popcorn Ceiling Removal Works

The same steps for one bedroom or every ceiling in the house.

1. Test Older Ceilings First

Popcorn applied before the 1980s can contain asbestos. If your home is from that period, we recommend testing by an accredited asbestos professional before anything is scraped. EPA homeowner guidance.

2. Cover the Room

Floors, walls, windows and furniture are covered and the work area is sealed in plastic before scraping starts.

3. Wet and Scrape

The popcorn is wetted and scraped off the ceiling down to the drywall.

4. Repair What Was Underneath

Popcorn hides tape joints, nail pops and old patches. We repair them and skim coat where the new finish needs it.

5. Apply the New Finish

Smooth, light knockdown or orange peel. We spray a sample first to dial in the finish before the whole ceiling is done.

6. Prime and Paint

Ceilings are primed and painted with ceiling paint, and the work area is cleaned up.

Finish Options After the Popcorn Comes Off

Smooth

Skim coated flat. It takes the most preparation because light across a smooth ceiling shows every variation.

Light Knockdown

The most common finish in newer DFW homes. It hides minor variation and matches many existing walls.

Orange Peel

A fine spray texture, often chosen to match orange peel walls.

Popcorn Ceiling Removal FAQ

How much does popcorn ceiling removal cost?

Popcorn removal is a drywall project, so it starts at our $1,000 drywall minimum. A whole-home job is quoted by the rooms and ceiling area, ceiling height, repairs found under the popcorn, the finish you choose and the paint. Send photos of each room for a free estimate.

Can old popcorn contain asbestos?

Popcorn applied before the 1980s can. If your home is from that period, we recommend testing by an accredited asbestos professional before any scraping.

Should I choose a smooth ceiling or knockdown?

Smooth takes more preparation because light across a flat ceiling shows every variation. Light knockdown and orange peel hide minor variation and match many DFW walls. We spray a sample first so you can decide.

Do you paint the ceilings after removal?

Yes. Primer and ceiling paint are part of the job. Wall repairs and repainting can be added to the same quote while the rooms are covered.

Can you do the whole house at once?

Yes. We quote the whole house and plan the order of rooms with you, especially if you are living there during the work. Tall and two-story ceilings are done from scaffold.

Should the ceilings be done before new floors?

Usually, yes. Doing the ceilings first keeps scraping and spraying off the new floors. If flooring and paint are part of the plan, we can quote the whole package together.

Do you work in The Colony, Lewisville and Little Elm?

Yes, through our second office, OnPoint Pros Remodeling at Grandscape in The Colony, which handles texture and popcorn removal in those cities.
