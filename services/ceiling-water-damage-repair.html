How We Rebuild a Water Damaged Ceiling

Paint over a stain while the drywall behind it is still wet and the stain comes back. That is why the order matters.

1. The Leak Gets Fixed First

A roofer, plumber or AC technician stops the water before we close anything. If you need a name, we can point you to trades we have worked beside.

2. Wet Drywall Comes Out

We cut the stained, sagging or soft drywall back to sound board and the nearest joists, so the new piece is fastened to framing.

3. Dry Before We Close It

We check that the framing and insulation are dry before the new drywall goes up. If something is still wet, the ceiling waits.

4. New Drywall, Taped and Finished

New board is hung, taped and finished, and any access panel or vent opening is framed so it sits clean.

5. Texture Matched on a Sample

Most DFW ceilings are knockdown or orange peel. We spray a sample first and feather the new texture wide so the repair blends. If the room has popcorn, this is a good time for popcorn ceiling removal across the whole ceiling.

6. Primed and Painted

A painted patch on a ceiling shows, so we prime and paint the whole ceiling or to a natural break, and the walls too when the water ran down them.

Ceiling Water Damage FAQ

How much does ceiling water damage repair cost?

Drywall projects start at $1,000. The price depends on how much drywall has to come out, the ceiling height, texture matching, and how much of the ceiling or wall gets painted. Send photos of every damaged area for a free estimate.

Can you repair the ceiling before the leak is fixed?

No. The roofer, plumber or AC technician fixes the leak first, and we check that the framing and insulation are dry before new drywall goes up. Closing a wet ceiling brings the stain back.

Do you work with insurance claims?

Yes. We rebuild the drywall and ceilings after the mitigation company has dried the house, and we price the work against your adjuster's scope. We do not do water extraction or mold removal.

Will the repair show after it is painted?

We spray the texture on a sample first, feather it wide into the existing ceiling, and paint the whole ceiling or to a natural break. That keeps a bright square from showing where the repair was.

What happens if the leak came from an upstairs bathroom?

We repair the ceiling below once the plumbing is fixed. If the shower or tub upstairs needs replacing, full bathroom remodels start at $18,000, and most land between $25,000 and $45,000. That starts with a quick call and a free in-home visit with Dillon.

Do you cover The Colony, Lewisville and Little Elm?

Yes, through our second office, OnPoint Pros Remodeling at Grandscape in The Colony, which handles ceiling and water damage repair in those cities.
