Materials That Actually Survive a DFW Climate

DFW summers are brutal on caulk, grout, and cheap fixtures. We use materials that hold up.

Large-Format Porcelain Tile

Fewer grout lines, easier to clean, and engineered for wet environments. Our default for shower walls and floors.

Quartz Countertops

Non-porous, doesn't need sealing, resistant to the makeup, toothpaste, and product stains that destroy marble.

Plywood-Box Vanities

We avoid particle-board and MDF cabinetry in wet rooms. Plywood-box construction handles humidity without swelling.

Schluter Waterproofing

Industry-standard membrane behind the tile so leaks never become a teardown two years later. We don't cut this corner.

Brass & Matte Black Fixtures

From Delta, Moen, Kohler, and Brizo. We avoid no-name fixtures because they're the #1 source of warranty calls.

Frameless Glass Enclosures

Custom-cut to your exact opening, with proper hinges and seals. No flimsy big-box kits.

Bathroom Remodel Questions DFW Homeowners Ask

How long does a bathroom remodel take in DFW?

Most bathroom remodels run 2 to 4 weeks depending on scope. A guest bath cosmetic refresh can be done in about 10 working days. A master bath gut with a shower-pan rebuild and full tile job typically lands at 3 to 4 weeks. Layout changes that move plumbing add another week.

Do I need a permit to remodel a bathroom in DFW?

It depends on the scope and your city. Cosmetic refreshes (vanity swap, paint, tile floor) usually don't. Anything that moves plumbing, alters framing, or touches electrical typically does. OnPoint Pros pulls every required permit on your behalf as part of our process — it's not your job to figure out city hall.

Can you convert a tub to a walk-in shower?

Yes — tub-to-shower conversions are one of the most common bathroom projects we do across DFW, especially for empty-nesters and aging-in-place homeowners. We rebuild the shower pan, retile, reroute drainage, and waterproof properly. It's a project we run almost every week.

Do you handle plumbing and electrical?

Yes. OnPoint Pros handles everything with our regular trade partners. You get one quote, one schedule, one project lead — never a relay race between separate contractors who blame each other when something goes wrong.

Will I be able to use the bathroom during the remodel?

If it's your only bathroom, we phase the work so you have a usable shower or toilet for as much of the project as possible. If you have a second bathroom, we recommend using it during demo and tile days because of dust and noise.

What's your warranty?

We provide a written workmanship warranty on every bathroom remodel. Manufacturer warranties on fixtures and materials are passed through. If something we installed goes wrong, we come fix it.
