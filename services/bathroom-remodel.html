How your bathroom project starts

Start with a conversation about the room, your priorities and investment. Detailed planning is a separate paid step, explained before you book it.

1

Initial planning call

Tell us what you want to change, where you live and when you'd like to begin. We discuss whether the full remodel service fits your goals and budget.

2

Paid Primary Bath Blueprint

The Blueprint develops your layout, selections and project scope. It usually costs $1,000, credited toward your remodel. We'll confirm the fee and what's included before you decide to book this planning step.

3

Agree on the scope and quote

Review a fixed quote for the agreed scope, your selections and the construction schedule. Any later changes need their own agreed scope and price.

4

Stage materials, then build

Prepare the materials before demolition, then move through the agreed work to the finished room. Discuss bathroom access and household arrangements before the project starts.

Before you plan your remodel

What should I budget for a full bathroom remodel?

Most OnPoint Pros full primary bathroom projects are $25,000–$45,000, with a $20,000 minimum. Room size, layout changes, tile, fixtures and the agreed scope affect the price. This range is a planning guide; your project receives its own quote.

Is the planning call the same as a Blueprint session?

No. The initial call is to discuss your project and fit. The Primary Bath Blueprint is the detailed paid planning step that follows if you decide to move ahead. It usually costs $1,000, credited toward your remodel. We'll explain the scope and fee before you book; requesting a call does not book or charge you for a Blueprint.

How long will my bathroom be out of use?

That depends on the agreed scope and construction schedule. We discuss the expected disruption and your access to another bathroom during planning. Ask about the schedule for your room before committing to construction.

Do you offer drywall, painting and smaller repairs?

Yes. Our $20,000 minimum applies to full bathroom remodels, not every service we offer. Drywall projects have a separate $1,000 minimum. For drywall, painting or interior repairs, request a repair quote and tell us what needs attention. Photos help us understand the work.

Which areas do you serve for bathroom remodeling?

Our primary bathroom service focuses on Allen, Plano, Frisco and McKinney. If you're in another North Dallas community, share your location during the planning call so we can confirm coverage for your project.

Need repairs before a full remodel?

We still help homeowners with drywall repair, painting and interior updates. Tell us about the work you need now.
