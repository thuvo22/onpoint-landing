DFW Subfloors Are Almost Never Flat. We Fix That First.

DFW homes built on slab over expansive clay soil rarely have a perfectly flat subfloor by the time you're ready to replace your flooring. Most "flooring guys" just ignore it and install over the dips — which is exactly why your floor creaks, why the click-lock pops, and why the tile telegraphs cracks within a year.

OnPoint Pros pulls the old floor, scans the subfloor with a level, and pours self-leveling underlayment where it needs it. It adds a day. It saves the floor.

Flooring FAQ

LVP or engineered hardwood?

For families with kids and pets, LVP wins on durability and water resistance. For resale value and "feel," engineered hardwood wins. We'll walk you through both at the estimate.

How long does a whole-house flooring install take?

A typical 2,500 sq ft DFW home runs 4 to 6 working days for LVP, 6 to 9 days for engineered hardwood, and longer for tile depending on prep.

Do you move furniture?

Yes — included for most projects. We move it, install, and put it back.
