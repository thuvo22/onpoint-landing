A Good Floor Starts With the Surface Underneath

Before choosing an installation plan, account for the existing floor, subfloor condition, transitions and the height of the new material. Uneven areas or moisture concerns can change the preparation needed.

For a larger flooring project, include connected rooms, hallways, stairs and closets in the measurement. Discuss removal, furniture, baseboards, door clearances and transitions in the written quote instead of treating them as afterthoughts.

We install luxury vinyl plank, hardwood, tile, laminate and carpet. The right product depends on the room, substrate, budget and manufacturer requirements. Bring your preferred material or tell us which options you want to compare.

What Flooring Costs in DFW

Flooring is priced per square foot, and the material sets the range. Carpet and laminate are the most budget-friendly. Luxury vinyl plank sits in the popular mid-range and is where most DFW homeowners land for the waterproof durability. Hardwood and tile are the premium end, both for material and labor.

The other cost factor is prep: if your slab needs leveling or you have old flooring to tear out and haul off, that's part of an honest quote. We measure, tell you the per-square-foot price by material, and include the prep so there are no surprises.

Flooring FAQ

How much does flooring installation cost in DFW?

Flooring is priced per square foot by material: laminate and carpet are most affordable, LVP is the popular mid-range, hardwood and tile are premium. We give a firm per-square-foot quote after measuring, including subfloor prep.

What's the best flooring for DFW homes?

For most homes with kids or pets, waterproof luxury vinyl plank (LVP) is the best value. Engineered hardwood handles North Texas humidity better than solid wood, and porcelain tile is best for wet areas.

Do you level the subfloor before installing?

Yes. DFW slabs are rarely flat because of expansive-clay soil movement. We check flatness and self-level or fix squeaks before installing, the step most installers skip.

Do you move furniture and remove the old floor?

Yes: furniture moving, tear-out, and haul-away of the old flooring are included so you're not left with the mess.

Is luxury vinyl plank really waterproof?

Quality LVP is 100% waterproof through the core, which is why it's so popular in DFW homes with kids and pets. It handles spills, mopping, and pet accidents far better than wood or laminate.
