Texture Matching Is Where Most DFW Drywall Crews Lose

A drywall patch is easy. A drywall patch you can't see in raking light from across the room is hard. DFW homes are almost all knockdown texture, and getting the spray pattern, knockdown blade angle, and dry time exactly right is the difference between an invisible patch and a Band-Aid that catches your eye every time you walk past.

OnPoint Pros repairs drywall across North Dallas every week — it's the single most common job we do, and it's what our neighbors review us for. We've matched DFW textures long enough to nail it on the first coat: orange peel, knockdown, splatter, smooth Level 5. Whatever your house has, we'll match it, then prime and paint so the repair disappears.

Drywall and sheetrock are the same thing — sheetrock is just the brand name most people use. Whether you call it drywall, sheetrock, wallboard, or gyp board, we cut it, hang it, tape it, mud it, sand it, and texture it clean.

What Drywall Repair Costs in DFW

Most homeowners want a number, so here's how pricing actually works. Small patches — a doorknob hole, a few anchor scars, a single crack — are the most affordable and often finish in one visit. Larger jobs scale with three things: the size of the damaged area, whether it's on a ceiling (harder and slower than a wall), and how much texture matching and painting it takes to blend in.

Water-damage and popcorn-ceiling jobs are quoted per room because they involve removal, drying, retaping, and refinishing. The honest answer is that a photo tells us most of what we need — text one over and we'll give you a clear, upfront price with no surprise add-ons.

Drywall Repair FAQ

How much does drywall repair cost in DFW?

Small patches typically start at a low flat rate. Larger holes, ceiling repair, water damage, and popcorn removal are quoted per job. Text us a photo and we give you an upfront price before any work begins.

Can you really make a drywall patch invisible?

In most cases, yes — when the paint matches and lighting is normal. We test the texture pattern on a sample first to dial in spray pressure and knockdown timing so the patch blends into the wall.

Do you match knockdown and orange-peel texture?

Yes. Almost all DFW homes are knockdown or orange peel, and texture matching is the core of what we do. We also do splatter and Level 5 smooth.

Do you paint after the drywall repair?

Yes — we can prime and paint the repaired area, or repaint the full wall corner-to-corner for a seamless color match.

How fast can you come out?

Most DFW drywall repair calls are scheduled within a few days, and many small patches are completed the same week. Water-damage and storm emergencies we prioritize.

What's the difference between drywall and sheetrock?

They're the same thing. Sheetrock is simply a brand name for gypsum drywall board that became the common term. We repair and install both — it's the same material.
