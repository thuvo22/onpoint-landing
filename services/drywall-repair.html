Texture Matching Is Where Most DFW Drywall Crews Lose

A drywall patch is easy. A drywall patch you can't see in raking light from across the room is hard. DFW homes are almost all knockdown texture, and getting the spray pattern, knockdown blade angle, and dry time exactly right is the difference between an invisible patch and a Band-Aid that catches your eye every time you walk past.

OnPoint Pros has been matching DFW textures long enough to nail it on the first coat — orange peel, knockdown, splatter, smooth Level 5. Whatever your house has, we'll match it.

Drywall FAQ

Can you really make a patch invisible?

In most cases, yes — when paint matches and lighting is normal. We test the texture pattern on a piece of cardboard first to dial in spray pressure and knockdown timing.

Do you also paint after the repair?

Yes — we can prime and paint the repaired area, or repaint the whole wall if you want a perfect color match.

How fast can you come out?

Most DFW drywall repair calls can be scheduled within a few days. Storm-damage emergencies we move faster.
