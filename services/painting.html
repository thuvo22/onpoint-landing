Start With the Walls, Then Choose the Finish

A fresh color looks better on a properly prepared surface. Include cracks, patches, rough texture and damaged trim in your painting request so preparation and painting can be quoted together.

For a multi-room or whole-home repaint, list which walls, ceilings, doors, closets and trim should be included. Tell us if furniture will remain in place and whether you plan to live in the home during the work. Confirm protection, access and room sequencing before the start date.

Photos help us understand the existing condition. Final preparation, paint products, color changes and the areas being painted belong in the written scope, so you can compare the full job rather than just a headline price.

Painting FAQ

How long does a whole-home interior repaint take?

A typical 2,500–3,500 sq ft DFW home runs 4 to 7 working days for a full interior repaint depending on prep needs and color count.

Do I need to move out?

No. We work room by room and protect everything. Most clients stay in the home throughout the project.

What paint do you use?

Sherwin-Williams and Benjamin Moore as defaults. We avoid contractor-grade lines because they don't hold up in DFW UV.

Can you spray our cabinets in place?

Yes: we set up a controlled spray zone, mask everything, and refinish cabinets without removing them from the house. Doors are sprayed off-site for a factory finish.
