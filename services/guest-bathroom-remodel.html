The full remodel scope, sized for a guest bath

A guest or hall bathroom goes through the same steps as any full-tile remodel. The room is smaller, so the decisions are about fitting the shower, storage and fixtures to the space and to the people who use it.

Tub or walk-in shower

Keep a tub for children or visitors, or replace it with a walk-in tile shower. See how a tub-to-shower conversion works.

A waterproofed tile shower

The walls and pan get a connected waterproofing system, and new tiled showers get a documented 24-hour flood test before the shower floor tile goes in.

Vanity and storage

A vanity sized to the room, plus a recessed niche in the shower so bottles stay off the floor and ledges.

Floor and wall tile

Tile is planned together, so the floor, the shower walls and the niche line up with each other.

Fixtures, glass and lighting

Shower valve and head, faucet, glass, exhaust fan and lights, with plumbing and electrical work by licensed master plumbers and licensed electricians.

Easier access when needed

Grab bar blocking, a bench or a low-curb entry for a parent or guest who needs them. See our curbless and accessible showers.

Planning a guest or hall bathroom

Start with who uses the room. A hall bath shared by children, a guest bath used a few times a year and a bathroom for a parent who is moving in each lead to different choices about the tub, the shower entry and storage.

Plan for the smaller footprint. In a small room, a glass panel instead of a curtain, a niche instead of a shelf and a vanity sized to the door swing help the room feel open. Our small bathroom remodel ideas cover more of these choices.

Plan around the rest of the house. Guest and hall baths often sit along a hallway or near bedrooms. Before demolition we discuss access, the path the crew uses through the house, how that area is protected and which bathroom you will use during the work.

Guest bathroom questions

How much does a guest bathroom remodel cost?

Guest and hall bathrooms are priced like our other full-tile bathroom remodels. Full bathroom remodels start at $18,000, and most run $25,000 to $45,000. Room size, whether you keep the tub, layout changes, tile and fixtures affect the price. You get a fixed written price before any work starts.

Is a guest bathroom remodel different from a primary bathroom remodel?

The steps are the same: demolition, plumbing and electrical work as needed, a waterproofed shower or tub surround, tile, vanity, fixtures and glass. The room is usually smaller, and the choices often center on whether to keep a tub and how much storage fits.

Should I keep the tub in a guest bathroom?

Keep it if children bathe in that room. If adults use the room most, a walk-in tile shower is easier to step into. We go over both layouts at the free in-home visit.

Do you remodel small bathrooms?

Yes. A small bathroom gets the same full-tile scope and the same $18,000 minimum as a larger one. In a small room, the choices that count are the layout, glass instead of a curtain, a recessed niche and a vanity sized to the space.

What happens at the free in-home visit?

Dillon measures the room, talks through your project and budget, and gives you a price range. If the project is a fit, we plan the layout and selections with you, and you get a fixed written price before any work starts.

Can you rebuild only the guest shower?

Our $18,000 minimum applies to full bathroom remodels. If you only want shower work, describe it when you contact us so we can confirm fit before we visit.
