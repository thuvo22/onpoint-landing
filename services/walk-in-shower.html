How we build a walk-in shower

Every walk-in shower we build follows the same sequence, and the written scope lists each step for your room. Shower sizes, glass styles, niches, benches and drain types are covered in our walk-in shower guide; at the visit, Dillon tells you which of them fit your room.

1. Demolition

The old tub or shower and its tile come out, and the walls and floor are checked before anything new goes in.

2. Plumbing

The licensed master plumbers we work with set the valve and drain for the new layout. Moving either one is planned and priced in the written scope.

3. Waterproofing

Cement backer board or approved foam board in the wet areas, a continuous sheet or liquid-applied membrane on the walls, niche, bench and curb, and a sloped pan with a waterproof liner connected to the drain.

4. 24-hour flood test

Before any shower floor tile goes in, we plug the drain, fill the pan and confirm the water level holds for 24 hours. The test is photographed for your job file.

5. Tile

Wall tile, floor tile, the niche and any bench are installed and grouted to the layout you approved.

6. Glass and fixtures

The glass door or panel, shower head and trim go in after the tile is finished.

Walk-in shower questions

How much does a walk-in shower remodel cost?

A walk-in shower is built as part of a full-tile bathroom remodel. Full bathroom remodels start at $18,000, and most run $25,000 to $45,000. Shower size, plumbing changes, tile, glass and the rest of the room's scope affect the price. You get a fixed written price before any work starts.

What does your walk-in shower work include?

Demolition, plumbing changes as needed, backer board or foam board, a connected waterproofing membrane, a sloped pan with a liner tied to the drain, a 24-hour flood test before the shower floor tile, tile and grout, then glass and fixtures. The written scope lists each item for your room.

How do you keep a walk-in shower from leaking?

Our tile crews build a connected waterproofing system behind the tile and under the shower floor. Before any shower floor tile goes in, we plug the drain, fill the pan and confirm the water level holds for 24 hours. The test is photographed for your job file, and you are welcome to watch it.

Can you replace my tub with a walk-in shower?

Yes. A tub-to-shower conversion is planned as part of the full bathroom remodel, including any drain and valve changes. See how our tub-to-shower conversions work.

Can a walk-in shower be curbless?

That depends on the floor structure and where the drain can go. When a level entry does not fit the room, a low curb keeps the step small. Dillon checks this during the free in-home visit. See curbless and accessible showers.

What warranty comes with a new shower?

Every full-tile bathroom remodel we build includes a written 3-year workmanship warranty that covers our tile work and the shower pan and waterproofing we installed. Faucets, fixtures, tile and glass carry their own manufacturer warranties. Read the full warranty terms.

Can you replace only the shower?

Our $18,000 minimum applies to full bathroom remodels. If you only want shower work, describe it when you contact us so we can confirm fit before we visit.
