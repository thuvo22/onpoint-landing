Prosper Homes Need a Different Standard. Here's Ours.

Prosper is one of North Dallas' most upscale, fastest-growing communities. The homes are mostly newer construction with custom millwork, custom cabinetry, and finish details most contractors don't know how to match. The crown molding has to be coped, not mitered. The cabinet finish has to be sprayed and laid down flat. The tile pattern has to line up at every seam.

OnPoint Pros runs Prosper projects with our most-experienced finish carpenter and tile crew because Prosper clients notice everything. That's not a complaint — it's the reason we love working there.
