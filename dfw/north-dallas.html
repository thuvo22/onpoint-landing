North Dallas Remodeling FAQ

Which North Dallas ZIP codes do you work in?

North central Dallas: 75205, 75225, 75230, 75231, 75240, 75243, 75248, 75251, 75252, 75254 and 75287. Northwest Dallas and Farmers Branch: 75209, 75220, 75229, 75234 and 75244. Central Dallas is outside our area.

What does a bathroom remodel cost in North Dallas?

Full bathroom remodels start at $18,000, and most land between $25,000 and $45,000. The first step is a quick call and a free in-home visit with Dillon, who measures the room and gives you a price range. You get a fixed written price before any work starts.

Do you repair ceilings after a leak?

Yes. Once the leak is fixed, we replace the wet drywall, match the texture and paint. Drywall projects start at $1,000, and we can price the rebuild against an insurance adjuster's scope.

Do you remove popcorn ceilings in older North Dallas homes?

Yes, one room or the whole house, finished smooth or with a new texture and painted. Popcorn applied before the 1980s can contain asbestos, so we recommend a test before any scraping.

Are you insured?

Yes. OnPoint Pros is family-owned and fully insured, based at 1305 Spring St in Allen.
