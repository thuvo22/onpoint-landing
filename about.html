About Us

Tile Bathrooms, With a Local Team

OnPoint Pros is a family-owned remodeling company based in Allen. Tile and full bathroom remodeling are our focus, with drywall repair, painting and interior updates available for homeowners across North Dallas.

Why We Started

Most DFW homeowners we talk to have the same horror story. They hired a contractor who showed up enthusiastic, took a deposit, and then... vanished. Or kept showing up but with a different crew every day. Or finished 80% of the job and disappeared on the punch list. Or quietly added "change orders" until the budget doubled.

Thu Vo and Dillon Hubbard, OnPoint Pros' founders, started this company because the bar in DFW remodeling is genuinely low, and homeowners deserve better. The whole company is built around one idea: the person you meet at the estimate is the person you call when something needs attention, from day one to final walkthrough.

One project lead stays in touch from planning through the final walkthrough. Bathroom projects begin with a planning call and, if the project fits, a paid Primary Bath Blueprint. Repairs have a separate scope review and quote.

How We're Different

  • ✓One point of contact from estimate to walkthrough, no handoffs.
  • ✓Written line-item quotes for the agreed construction scope; the paid Blueprint is a separate planning step.
  • ✓Daily progress photos straight to your phone.
  • ✓Project terms in writing, including the approved scope, schedule and applicable warranty.
  • ✓Locally owned and based in Allen, not a national franchise.

Where We Work

We work across the entire North Dallas metroplex including Frisco, Plano, McKinney, Allen, Prosper, The Colony, Little Elm, McKinney, Allen, Prosper, and Dallas.
