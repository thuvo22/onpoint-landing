About Us

Tile Bathrooms, With a Local Team

OnPoint Pros is a family-owned remodeling company based in Allen. Tile and full bathroom remodeling are our focus, with drywall repair, painting and interior updates available for homeowners across North Dallas.

A Bathroom Team Based in Allen

Founded by Thu Vo and Dillon Hubbard, OnPoint Pros focuses on full-tile primary bathrooms and custom tile showers. We help homeowners plan the shower, tile, vanity, fixtures and finishing details as one agreed scope.

One project lead stays in touch from planning through the final walkthrough. Bathroom projects begin with a planning call and, if the project fits, a paid Primary Bath Blueprint. Drywall, painting and interior repairs have a separate scope review and quote.

What You Can Expect

  • Written scope and pricing: confirm selections, inclusions and the construction quote before work begins.
  • Licensed trades: we work with licensed master plumbers and licensed electricians.
  • Documented shower testing: new tiled showers receive a 24-hour flood test before shower floor tile is installed.
  • Permit coordination: when permits are required, we handle applications and required inspections.
  • 3-year written workmanship warranty: every full-tile bathroom remodel includes coverage for installation-caused failures, subject to the coverage and exclusions.

Where We Work

Our primary bathroom service focuses on Allen, Plano, Frisco and McKinney. For another North Dallas community, share your address and project during the planning call so we can confirm availability.

Our Promise

We put the agreed scope and price in writing, communicate during the job and review any changes before additional work proceeds. Read our shower waterproofing process, permit responsibilities and full bathroom warranty terms.
