About Us

A DFW Contractor That Actually Picks Up the Phone

OnPoint Pros is a locally owned, family-owned North Dallas remodeling company. Based in Allen. Run by people who live here.

Why We Started

Most DFW homeowners we talk to have the same horror story. They hired a contractor who showed up enthusiastic, took a deposit, and then... vanished. Or kept showing up but with a different crew every day. Or finished 80% of the job and disappeared on the punch list. Or quietly added "change orders" until the budget doubled.

Dillon Hubbard, OnPoint Pros' founder, started this company because the bar in DFW remodeling is genuinely low — and homeowners deserve better. The whole company is built around one idea: the person you meet at the estimate is the person you call when something needs attention, from day one to final walkthrough.

No call centers. No relay race between subcontractors. No mystery charges. One trusted local crew handling kitchens, bathrooms, painting, flooring, drywall, and handyman work across the entire DFW metroplex.

How We're Different

  • ✓One point of contact from estimate to walkthrough — no handoffs.

Where We Work

We work across the entire North Dallas metroplex including Frisco, Plano, McKinney, Allen, Prosper, The Colony, Little Elm, McKinney, Allen, Prosper, and Dallas.

Our Promise

If we quote it, we build it. If we say we'll be there Tuesday, we're there Tuesday. If something we installed fails, we come fix it.

That's not a marketing line — it's the reason we get most of our work from neighbor-to-neighbor referrals across DFW.
