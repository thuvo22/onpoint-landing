Carrollton Bathroom Remodel FAQ

How much does a bathroom remodel cost in Carrollton?

A guest-bath refresh is the most affordable; a full master bath with a custom walk-in shower is a larger investment. We give a written, line-item quote after a free on-site visit so you see exactly where the money goes.

Do you do tub-to-shower conversions in Carrollton?

Yes — it's one of our most popular Carrollton projects, especially for empty-nesters and aging-in-place. We remove the old tub and build a custom walk-in shower in its place.

How long does a Carrollton bathroom remodel take?

A guest-bath refresh is about two weeks; a full master bath with shower-pan rebuild and tile runs three to four weeks. Moving plumbing adds time.

Do you pull permits in Carrollton?

Yes. Anything that moves plumbing or alters framing typically needs a permit, and we pull it on your behalf as part of the project.
