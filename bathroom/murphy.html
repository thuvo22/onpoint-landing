Murphy Bathroom Remodeling FAQ

How much does a bathroom remodel cost in Murphy?

Most OnPoint Pros full-tile primary bathroom remodels are $25,000–$45,000, with a $20,000 minimum. Your room, layout, selections and agreed scope determine the final quote. Start with an initial planning call. If you choose to move into detailed planning, the Primary Bath Blueprint usually costs $1,000, credited toward your remodel. We confirm the fee and what is included before you book it. Requesting a call does not book or charge you for a Blueprint.

Do you do tub-to-shower conversions in Murphy?

A tub-to-shower conversion can be planned as part of your full primary bathroom remodel. We review the layout, tile, glass and other selections together. The $20,000 minimum applies to the full remodel.

How long does a bathroom remodel in Murphy take?

The construction schedule depends on your agreed scope, layout changes and selections. We discuss the expected disruption and access to another bathroom during planning, and stage materials before demolition. Confirm the schedule for your project before construction begins.

Do you pull permits in Murphy?

Yes. OnPoint Pros checks Murphy requirements and handles required permits and inspections. We work with licensed master plumbers and licensed electricians. Expected permit and inspection fees appear as a separate line item in your quote and are included in the total before you sign. See permit and trade details.

Do you provide a written workmanship warranty?

Yes. Every full-tile bathroom remodel includes a 3-year written workmanship warranty. We repair covered installation-caused failures at our cost. Read the coverage, exclusions and separate manufacturer warranty terms.

Do you flood-test new tiled showers?

Yes. For new tiled showers, our tile crews perform and photo-document a 24-hour flood test before installing shower floor tile. See how our shower waterproofing and testing work.
